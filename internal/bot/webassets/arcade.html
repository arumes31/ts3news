The archivist's challenge

Reveal two tiles per move. Matching relics stay face up; mismatches turn back over. Clear all eight pairs in as few moves as possible. Time breaks a tie. The clock starts on your first reveal. Use the arrow keys, Tab and Enter, or tap a tile. Pause stops the clock and conceals the board; switching tabs pauses automatically. Starting again resets the board.
